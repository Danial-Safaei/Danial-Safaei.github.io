Research experience

Oct 2024 – now

PhD Researcher

Safe Autonomy Research Group, WMG, University of Warwick

  • Created Decisive-Feature Fidelity (DFF), a realism measure that compares the evidence a frozen perception network uses on a real image and on its synthetic counterpart, estimated with counterfactual explanations (IEEE IV 2026).
  • Extended it to the concept level (C-DFF), which shows which scene concepts a network relies on differently in simulation, evaluated on 2,126 exact-geometry KITTI and Virtual KITTI 2 pairs with three frozen networks (IEEE ITSC 2026).
  • Full-length study (preprint, arXiv:2611.27447, November 2026): a benchmark of ten retrained ControlNet generators and three frozen networks, showing that output agreement and image similarity can miss differences in the evidence a network uses.
  • Statistical evaluation throughout: pair-clustered bootstrap confidence intervals, permutation tests, positive controls and held-out replication. Open-source code: github.com/Danial-Safaei/DFF.
