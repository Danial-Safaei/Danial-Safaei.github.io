Research at a glance

Decisive Feature Fidelity · IEEE IV 2026

Same output, different reason. A real frame and its synthetic twin can produce identical outputs from the same frozen model while relying on different visual evidence. DFF makes that divergence measurable — so fidelity is judged by why the system decides, not how the images look.
